# Reel scripts: Fatherhood / break the cycle week (Cory records; talking to camera, 15-30 sec, captions on screen)

Blog: "How to Break the Cycle of Fatherlessness: What We Choose to Hand Down" (Sunday Nov 22). Record Saturday. No comment/tag/send prompts. Use only stories that are true for Cory; the lines below are written as general truths, so swap in a real moment if he wants.

## Reel A: "No one at the table will tell you this"
- ON-SCREEN HOOK (first second): "No one at the table will say this, but I will."
- SPOKEN FIRST LINE: "This Thursday, some of us are going to look at an empty chair."
- TALKING POINTS:
  1. Maybe a father left, or was there and gone. Maybe we are the one who left.
  2. A cycle gets handed down by what we watch, and what is learned can be unlearned.
  3. It ends when one person starts showing up, again and again.
- CLOSING LINE: "The cycle can stop with you."
- Caption: How to break the cycle of fatherlessness starts with one honest decision and a lot of small, steady ones. Full post on our website. Link in bio. #fatherhood #generationalcycles #christianfather #secondchances

## Reel B: "3 things a father can do that cost nothing"
- ON-SCREEN HOOK: "3 things a father can do that cost nothing. Number 3 costs your ego."
- SPOKEN FIRST LINE: "Kids don't remember the big gestures. They remember the pattern."
- TALKING POINTS:
  1. Show up when you said you would.
  2. Put the phone down and look them in the eye.
  3. Say sorry first, and mean it.
- CLOSING LINE: "Small and steady beats big and rare."
- Caption: Simple fatherhood habits that build trust with your kids. Full post on our website. Link in bio. #fatherhood #parentingtips #accountability #christianfather
