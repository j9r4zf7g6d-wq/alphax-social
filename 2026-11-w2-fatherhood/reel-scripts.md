# Reel Scripts, Week of Nov 8 (Fatherhood). Cory records both (15-30 sec, talk to camera, captions on screen)

## Reel 1: "5 things your kids need from you that cost nothing"
- On-screen hook (first second): "5 things your kids need from you that cost $0. Number 5 might sting."
- Spoken first line: "Your kids don't need a bigger paycheck from you. They need these five things."
- Talking points:
  1. Your time, with the phone down. Your word, kept. Your calm when you're angry.
  2. Your love said out loud, because a lot of us never heard it.
  3. Number 5: ownership. Saying "that was on me, I'm sorry" is the strongest thing a father can say.
- Closing line: "The cycle stops with one dad deciding. Send this to a dad who's trying."
- Keyword to say early: "how to be a better father"
- Suggested hashtags (3): #fatherhood #christiandad #breakthecycle

## Reel 2: "No one at your kid's graduation will tell you this, but I will"
- On-screen hook: "No one will tell you this about being a dad, but I will."
- Spoken first line: "Providing is not the whole job."
- Talking points:
  1. A kid can live in a full house and still feel alone in it.
  2. They won't remember what you earned. They'll remember if you showed up and if they felt safe telling you the truth.
  3. It's not too late. You can't rewrite the past, but you have full authority over today.
- Closing line: "Show up today. Then send this to a dad who needs to hear it."
- Keyword to say early: "breaking generational cycles as a father"
- Suggested hashtags (3): #fatherhood #christianfather #accountability
