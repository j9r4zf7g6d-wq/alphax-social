# Reel scripts: Identity 2.0 week (Cory records; talking to camera, 15-30 sec, captions on screen)

Note: this week's scheduled Reels (Mon, Wed, Fri) are text-on-dark videos already in Metricool. These two are optional face-to-camera upgrades; swap in whichever Cory records.

## Reel A: "The church hurt you. God didn't."
- ON-SCREEN HOOK (first second): "The church hurt you. God didn't."
- SPOKEN FIRST LINE: "If the church hurt you, I'm not going to tell you it was small."
- TALKING POINTS:
  1. People can be inconsistent. God is faithful.
  2. I trusted the building, the pastor, the program. People are fallible, and so was I.
  3. Don't let it cost you twice: keep your faith in Him, not in the room.
- CLOSING LINE: "You are more than what happened to you."
- Caption: Healing from church hurt starts with separating people from God. Full post, Identity 2.0, on our website. Link in bio. #churchhurt #faithandhealing #identity

## Reel B: "3 things reconciliation is not"
- ON-SCREEN HOOK: "3 things reconciliation is NOT. Number 3 might sting."
- SPOKEN FIRST LINE: "Three things reconciliation is not, and the third one is on you."
- TALKING POINTS:
  1. It's not pretending it didn't happen.
  2. It's not a deadline, and it's not walking into a building before you're ready.
  3. It's not blaming yourself. You can start with one honest conversation with God.
- CLOSING LINE: "You don't have to walk into a church today. Start right where you are."
- Caption: How to heal after being hurt by church people: go at your own pace. #reconciliation #christianencouragement #healingjourney
